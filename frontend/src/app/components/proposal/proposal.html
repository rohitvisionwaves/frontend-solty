Dil ka sawaal, sirf ek baar—
Isi pal ki raah dekhe nazaara.

On this Valentine’s Day,
Will you be my Valentine?

"Na jaane kab tum meri zarurat ban gayi,
Har khushi ki wajah aur aadat ban gayi.
Aaj bas itna sa sawaal hai dil se,
Kya tum meri Valentine banogi?"

You just made me the happiest person. ❤️

"Mera dil, meri jaan, sab tumhare liye,
Ab har pal har lamha hum saath jiye."

Thank you for being honest.
Our friendship will always be special.

"Dosti hi sahi, par sath toh hoga,
Mere dil mein hamesha tumhara naam hoga."
